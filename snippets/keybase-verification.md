# Keybase verification

Use this template to point readers to an organization's Keybase identity and
public proof. A filled-in template is a reference, not itself a Keybase proof.

- **Organization:** [official organization name]
- **Keybase username:** [username]
- **Profile:** [https://keybase.io/username]
- **Public proof:** [link to the proof on its authoritative platform]
- **Claim verified by the proof:** [the exact identity or account being linked]
- **Checked:** [YYYY-MM-DD]

Before publishing, open the profile and proof and confirm that the proof is
valid, current, and connects the expected accounts.
